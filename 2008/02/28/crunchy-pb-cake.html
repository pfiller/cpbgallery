Crunchy Milk Chocolate PB Layer Cake

February 28, 2008

Is today your birthday? I bet you wish it was just so someone would make this cake for you.

It’s called the Crunchy Milk Chocolate–Peanut Butter Layer Cake and was adapted from Food and Wine Magazine by Joe of Culinary in the Desert.

The cake’s anatomy: Chocolate cake. A peanut butter filling that has milk chocolate, almonds, roasted peanuts and rice krispies. Topped with milk chocolate ganache.
