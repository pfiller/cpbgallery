Spread Peanut Butter

February 15, 2008

Spread is a restaurant in San Diego, California that serves “nouveau comfort food” and sells it’s own brand of flavored peanut butters. C&PB flavors include cookies & cream, dark chocolate chili, dark chocolate espresso & dark chocolate infused with pretzels.
