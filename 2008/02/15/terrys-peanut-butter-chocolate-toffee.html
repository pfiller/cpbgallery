Terry's Peanut Butter Chocolate Toffee

February 15, 2008

A few years ago before Terry’s Toffee had their own store, I had the good fortune of meeting the owner, Terry Opalek while he was doing a tasting at Southport Grocery in Chicago. Over bites of delicious toffee, he answered questions I had about starting your own sweets business. Visit his site or his shop in Chicago and grab yourself a bag of his Peanut Butter Chocolate Toffee. They’ve even been given as gifts at the Oscars!
