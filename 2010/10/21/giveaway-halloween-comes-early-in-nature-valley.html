Giveaway! Halloween Comes Early in Nature Valley

October 21, 2010

Hey there, I’m back! I’m happy to return to the blog, though a little sad to report that I only found 1 single jar of peanut butter over the course of two whole weeks in Spain. My European readers weren’t kidding when they said it was hard to find! On the bright side at least the spanish like their chocolate and pastries, so we found plenty of those.

I have a bunch to share about the trip but for now I’m going to celebrate my return to the US (and its readily available peanut butter) with my very first chocolate peanut butter giveaway! How exciting! And it’s not from just anyone - we’re giving away goodies from one of my favorite snack companies: Nature Valley. Yum!!

Nature Valley, maker of super-tasty granola bars was kind enough to share two excellent new products with me. They recently released these Granola Thins, which come in both peanut butter and dark chocolate.

Nature Valley makes healthy and convenient snacks that are really tasty and satisfying. I am one of those people that can - I will admit - get a little cranky if hunger strikes when there’s no food in sight, and their bars (safely stashed in my purse) have saved me on plenty of occasions.

What makes Granola Thins different than other Granola Bars? These Granola Thins are a little different than the typical log-shaped granola bars that you’re used to. Crunchy, nutty and packed with oats, they are similar in texture to Nature Valley’s Oats ‘N Honey Crunchy Granola Bars, but thinner and lined with a layer of either rich dark chocolate or luscious peanut butter. It’s a satisfying, low-calorie snack with a little indulgence.
